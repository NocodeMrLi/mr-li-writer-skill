# Open Source Acknowledgements

Mr.Li Writer is independently designed and maintained. The following open-source projects informed parts of its workflow and layout capabilities.

## human-writing

- Project: `human-writing` (活人感写作)
- Source: https://github.com/KKKKhazix/human-writing
- License: MIT

Mr.Li Writer adapted several editorial ideas at the workflow level: checking material sufficiency before drafting, establishing a speaking position, distinguishing reality from fiction, making every paragraph add information or action, drafting positively before review, and inspecting rhetorical actions rather than relying only on forbidden-word lists. These ideas were reworked into this project's adaptive editorial routing and lint rules; the original Skill and checker are not vendored here.
